# Military Institute of Science and Technology (MIST)

## About MIST

Military Institute of Science and Technology (MIST) is a leading technical university in Bangladesh, established in 1998. MIST is committed to providing quality education in science, technology, and engineering disciplines.

## Vision
To be a world-class institute of higher learning in science and technology, developing competent human resources for the nation.

## Mission
- Provide quality education in science and technology
- Conduct research and development activities
- Serve the nation through technological advancement
- Develop ethical and competent professionals

## Academic Programs

### Undergraduate Programs
- Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
- Electrical, Electronic and Communication Engineering (EECE)
- Mechanical Engineering (ME)
- Civil Engineering (CE)
- Aeronautical Engineering (AE)
- Naval Architecture and Marine Engineering (NAME)
- Nuclear and Radiation Engineering (NRE)
- Environmental, Water Resources and Coastal Engineering (EWRCE)
- Architecture (ARCH)


### Graduate Programs
- M.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering
- M.Sc. in Electrical, Electronic and Communication Engineering
- M.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering
- M.Sc. in Civil Engineering

### PhD Programs
PhD programs are available in most engineering disciplines.

## Admission Information

### Undergraduate Admission
- Admission test is conducted annually
- Eligibility: HSC or equivalent with minimum GPA requirements
- Subjects: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics for engineering programs
- Application deadline: Usually in August-September
- Admission test: September-October

### Graduate Admission
- Admission tests are conducted twice a year
- Requirements vary by program
- Bachelor's degree required for Master's programs
- Master's degree required for PhD programs
